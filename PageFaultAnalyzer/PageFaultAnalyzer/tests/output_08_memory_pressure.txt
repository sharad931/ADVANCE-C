--- Interpretation (real-world context) ---
  * Memory pressure shows what happens when far more pages are in use than there
    are frames: pages are evicted before they can be reused.
  * Fault rate of the best practical policy is 70.0%: substantial. More frames
    would probably reduce faults noticeably (see the frame-capacity experiment).
  * FIFO had 2 fewer faults than LRU on this input: recency was not a good
    predictor of reuse here (this is allowed - LRU is not always better).
  * Optimal needed 43 faults; the best practical policy is 13 faults above this
    benchmark. Optimal is theoretical: it needs future knowledge that a real OS
    does not have.
  * Note: this is a page-replacement simulator. Real systems also involve caching,
    working-set tracking, storage latency and allocation policies that are not
    modelled here.
