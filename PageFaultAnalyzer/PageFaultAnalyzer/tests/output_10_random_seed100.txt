--- Interpretation (real-world context) ---
  * Random access resembles many unrelated applications or tabs: there is little
    pattern for any policy to exploit.
  * Fault rate of the best practical policy is 80.0%: very high. The 3 frames are
    far too few for this workload; a real system would be waiting on storage most
    of the time (thrashing).
  * FIFO had 1 fewer faults than LRU on this input: recency was not a good
    predictor of reuse here (this is allowed - LRU is not always better).
  * Optimal needed 31 faults; the best practical policy is 9 faults above this
    benchmark. Optimal is theoretical: it needs future knowledge that a real OS
    does not have.

    working-set tracking, storage latency and allocation policies that are not
    modelled here.
